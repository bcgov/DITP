# Digital Services Card Ecosystem

## Overview

The Digital Services Card ecosystem provides digital credentials for verifying individual identities and attributes in British Columbia. These credentials form the foundation of digital identity verification and enable secure, privacy-preserving interactions.

## Available Credentials

### BC Digital Services Card
The [BC Digital Services Card](./digital-services-card.md) serves as a foundational identity credential that:

- Verifies individual identity attributes
- Enables secure access to government services
- Protects privacy through selective disclosure
- Supports interoperability with other digital credentials
